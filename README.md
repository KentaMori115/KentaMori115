### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a backend and systems engineer with **8+ years** of experience building high-throughput distributed systems in **Rust** and **Go**, including **5+ years** on blockchain infrastructure (Ethereum, Cosmos SDK, Solana).

My core specialty is **performance engineering for compute-heavy workloads**. I have 10+ years of focused work optimizing elliptic curve cryptography, including finite field arithmetic, scalar multiplication, and massively parallel **GPU (CUDA)** computation. These are the same skills behind fast, cost-efficient AI training and inference.

I'm now applying that background to AI systems:

- **Model fine-tuning at scale.** Running fine-tuning experiments on Tongyi-MAI's 6B-parameter **Z-Image** base model across **1×, 4×, and 8× H100 SXM** setups, comparing training parameters, throughput, and cost.
- **Applied ML in production.** Deepfake detection and real-time anomaly detection systems, deployed as APIs.
- **Confidential and verifiable AI.** Exploring how my experience with FHE, zk-SNARKs, and Intel SGX remote attestation applies to private and verifiable AI workloads.
